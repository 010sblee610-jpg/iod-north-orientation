Why you're here

Ground Operations Command (GOC) keeps watch along the DMZ in peacetime. In a fight, it becomes the Combined Ground Component Command (CGCC) — the ROK-US combined element that would fight the DPRK. You're here to help build that combined element for UFS26.

IOD — Interoperability Directorate, 8th Army. Integrates the combined battlestaff. CGCC — Combined Ground Component Command. Where you work. CBS — Combined Battle Staff. That's you, working with your ROK counterpart.

07

Safety & Barracks Rules

Straight from the UFS26 brief — know these before your first night.

There is a 24-hour CQ. In an emergency, notify CQ — located in the middle of the first floor.
Barracks

No alcohol, no food or drinks

Do not consume alcohol in the barracks. No food or drinks are allowed in the barracks either.

Fire

If the alarm sounds

Exit the front of the barracks immediately and group up at the tennis courts. CQ will sweep the barracks and take accountability.

Fire Drill

HQ fire drill

Evacuate in an orderly fashion. Highest rank present gains accountability and reports to the Mayor Cell.

Environment

Trash & recycling

There are plenty of trash cans and recycle bins around post — use them to reduce wildlife and keep things clean.

Medical — PACE Plan
Minor injury/illness (Category C): GOC Medical Clinic, 0600–0700 & 1700–1800, or at time of injury. Sick call at the Combined Medical Clinic, B4 of GOC.

Beyond clinic capability (Category B): Camp Humphreys Emergency Room, Yongin Severance Hospital, Ajou University Hospital, or Dankook University Medical Center. If taken to a Korean hospital, you'll be accompanied by HQ KATUSA.

Life / limb / eyesight emergency (Category A): Evacuated to the nearest trauma center by ambulance, CASEVAC with TMP and a medic, or Air MEDEVAC (Humphreys).

Yongin Severance Hospital — 14 min · Ajou University Hospital — 25 min
